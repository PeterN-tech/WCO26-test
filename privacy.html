The Game does not require registration or login. We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

We may collect limited non-personal information automatically, including:

The Game uses your browser's local storage to save game progress (Quest mode, leaderboard scores, and settings). This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Any automatically collected data is used solely to:

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

We implement reasonable measures to protect any information processed through the Game. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
